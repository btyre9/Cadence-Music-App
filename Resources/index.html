LET YOUR DISCOVERIES GROW

Automatic listening history

Share what you listen to in Cadence with ListenBrainz. A listen counts after half the track or four minutes, whichever comes first. Tracks under 30 seconds and detected previews are skipped.

Your ListenBrainz history is public. Cadence sends track and artist names, album when available, service link, duration, and listening timestamp. Your token stays in macOS Keychain.

No account connected

Tracking is off.
Only playback inside Cadence is tracked. Recommendations refresh on ListenBrainz’s schedule; changes are not immediate. Turning tracking off keeps queued listens on this Mac; disconnecting removes them. Uploads already sent cannot be recalled.
On this Mac

Your ListenBrainz username and saved discoveries are stored locally. Listening credentials never pass through a Cadence server. Track matching opens a YouTube Music search for you to choose the recording in Cadence.
